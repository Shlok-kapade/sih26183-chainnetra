### ⚠️ Ground-Truth Bias Caveat

Recall is measured only against **labeled** VASP addresses in `data/labels/*.csv`. Unlabeled exchanges are invisible to evaluation: the engine will find more exits in practice, but they cannot be verified against ground truth without broader labeling. **Published recall figures are lower bounds on true investigative coverage.** Additionally, all results are from synthetic graphs (generators match training distribution); real-world graphs have partial topology, more diverse structures, and API budget constraints that differ from simulation.
