# ChainNetra Ethical Guidelines

ChainNetra is a powerful tool designed to aid Law Enforcement Agencies (LEAs) and compliance teams. We adhere to the following ethical principles:

1. **Probable Cause vs. Determinism:**
   Our AI models (e.g., LightGBM risk scorer) are intended strictly for *triage and lead generation*. They indicate probability, not absolute truth. They should never be used as the sole basis for an arrest or asset seizure without human corroboration and hard cryptographic evidence.

2. **Explainability First:**
   Black-box ML models are unacceptable in a legal context. We rely on SHAP values and heuristic rule IDs to ensure that every flag generated by the system can be explained to a judge, jury, or defense attorney in plain language.

3. **Data Privacy:**
   ChainNetra processes public blockchain data. It does not actively seek to deanonymize everyday users unless their wallets intersect with known illicit entities based on high-confidence taint paths.

4. **Bias Mitigation:**
   The training data (e.g., Elliptic++) was curated carefully with a strict temporal split to ensure the model doesn't overfit to specific past threat actors, maintaining fairness when evaluating new addresses.
